Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14.

Who uses this app. Teknium Hermes is used only by its owner. No other users are served.

Data accessed. With the owner's consent, the app reads the owner's Gmail messages and metadata through the read-only scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly.

Use. Data is used only to answer the owner's own requests (searching, reading, and summarizing his email). It is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used to train generalized AI/ML models.

Storage. OAuth tokens and any retrieved data are stored only on the owner's local computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited Use. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact. github.com/n0geegee.
